A Letter From Your Sister

My dearest Kubra,

Today you turn twenty-one, and my heart feels too full to hold everything I want to say. I still remember the little girl who followed me everywhere, the laughter we shared in quiet rooms, and the late-night talks where the world felt small enough to fit between two sisters. Watching you grow into the strong, gentle and beautiful woman you are has been one of the greatest blessings of my life.

You have a rare kindness. You notice people when they need it, you forgive quickly, and you carry light into places that are tired. I have learned so much from you without you ever trying to teach me. Thank you for being patient with me, for standing beside me, and for loving me even on the days I did not deserve it.

I pray that Allah gives you a life full of ease, health that never fails, and a heart that always feels at peace. May every door that opens for you lead to something good, and every tear you shed be replaced by a reason to smile. I want you to know that you are never alone, and that wherever life takes you, my love will find its way to you.

Having you in my life is a gift I thank Allah for every single day. Happy 21st birthday, my little sister, my best friend, my heart.

Forever yours, with all my love,
Your sister
